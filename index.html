About

I am a PhD researcher in the James Watt School of Engineering at the University of Glasgow. My work spans power-system protection, low-carbon energy systems, renewable forecasting, and optimisation, with an emphasis on methods that can be translated into practical engineering studies.

Focus

Research

AI for Power Systems

Machine-learning methods for fault detection, classification, protection, and operational intelligence in modern distribution networks.

Renewable Energy Forecasting

Long-horizon photovoltaic power forecasting using deep learning, generative modelling, and data-quality-aware pipelines.

Multi-Energy Systems

Optimisation of coupled electricity, hydrogen, thermal, and storage systems under economic, carbon, and reliability objectives.

Data-Centre Power Systems

AC/DC architectures, power-electronic interfaces, energy storage, and capacity-aware operation for high-density computing loads.
